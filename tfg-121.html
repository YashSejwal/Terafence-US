Overview · TFG-121

Data flows out, nothing comes back in

The TFG-121 is a unidirectional secure gateway and full protocol breaker with an FPGA data diode, in a DIN rail / desktop version with a fixed internal configuration.

It is placed between two segments (OT / IT) and moves data one way only. It acts as a full protocol breaker and proxy server, for Air-Gap-level segmentation.

Its core security technology is proprietary Terafence FPGA hardware with galvanic isolation. The security core has no OS, MAC or IP.

It is positioned as the central OT-to-IT boundary in control rooms and data centers.

Use cases · TFG-121

Where the TFG-121 fits best

Best for

  1. 01Substations / power utilities: one-way IEC-104 or Modbus from RTUs / IEDs to SCADA or historian, on DC station power
  2. 02Remote / harsh sites: pipelines, pump and lift stations, wellheads, water / wastewater, renewables
  3. 03Protecting individual PLCs, HMIs and IIoT sensors; containing infected devices
  4. 04Edge CCTV streams to VMS
  5. 05IIoT to cloud via MQTT / HTTPS

Who it’s built for

Utilities, oil and gas, water, transportation field engineering

When to consider another model

  • Two-way control
  • Rack-mount data-center installs
  • AC-only sites without DC supply
