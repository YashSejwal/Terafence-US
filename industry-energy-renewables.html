Why now

Why Generation and Fleet Operators Are Rethinking the Perimeter

Distributed clean energy multiplied remote entry points overnight. In December 2025, wiper malware reached more than 30 wind and solar sites in Poland through exposed edge VPN appliances, blinding site monitoring and severing plant-to-grid telemetry.

Regulatory scrutiny is tightening in parallel. NERC CIP-003-9 expands access-control discipline across low-impact bulk-electric assets and inverter-based resources (≥20 MVA) — explicitly citing a unidirectional gateway in Attachment 2 as evidence that prohibits inbound access — while NIS2, IEEE 1547.3, and state critical-infrastructure rules such as Texas SB 2116 restrict external vendor pathways.

How it works

Hardware Unidirectionality — Without Multi-Server Complexity

Inside one appliance, Terafence pairs isolated Side A and Side B proxy computers across a direction-locked FPGA core with no IP or MAC address on the data path. Your SCADA gateway or plant historian ends its TCP session on Side A; Side B initiates a clean session to enterprise, cloud, or ISO/RTO receivers. Nothing is installed on turbines, inverters, or DCS controllers, and no external proxy servers are required.
