  1. 01
    What we build

    Hardware that lets data leave and nothing return

    Terafence builds FPGA-based data diodes and unidirectional gateways. They keep essential operations connected to the data they produce, with network isolation enforced in hardware across OT, ICS, IoT and IT systems.

  2. 02
    Where it began

    Engineered in Israel since 2015

    Terafence was founded in 2015 in Israel, where its engineers design the FPGA logic. Its first FPGA data diode reached the market in 2017, and the hardware is manufactured to industrial grade in Taiwan.

  3. 03
    Where it runs

    On six continents, and inside other products

    Terafence gateways protect operations on six continents. More than 2,500 OEM machines carry Terafence hardware, reporting to their makers without opening a path into the customer's plant.

How we think

Three ideas behind every Terafence product

01

Every route out is a candidate route in

A connection built to send data out can be used to send something in. Terafence removes that possibility instead of policing it.

02

Enforce it in silicon, below the network stack

Firewalls decide what to let in. Terafence removes the inbound path in hardware, at Layers 1 and 2. The enforcing core has no operating system, no MAC address and no IP address.

03

Lose a reading. Never lose control

With no reverse circuit to open, the worst outcome is a missed reading, not a compromised process.
