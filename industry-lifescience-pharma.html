Why now

Where Cyber Exposure Collides with GxP Validation

In pharmaceutical, biologics, and medtech plants, an IT ransomware spill — like the NotPetya outbreak that cost a global vaccine manufacturer over $1.3 billion in lost output and recovery — threatens far more than IT uptime: it triggers deviation investigations, batch holds, and drug shortages.

At the same time, FDA 21 CFR Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11, and ALCOA+ require complete, unaltered electronic records, while NIS2 brings life-science manufacturers under strict OT security rules. Because qualified skids and legacy lab PCs cannot absorb constant endpoint agents or emergency OS patches without costly IQ/OQ/PQ re-validation, isolating them in hardware is the cleanest path.

How it works

Agentless Boundary Protection for Qualified Equipment

Terafence operates entirely at the network boundary — zero software is installed on PLC skids, chromatography workstations, or validated HMIs. Within a single chassis, Side A terminates local production or lab sessions and relays data across a direction-locked FPGA core (no IP or MAC address on the data path) to Side B, which delivers verified files and streams to enterprise quality systems.
