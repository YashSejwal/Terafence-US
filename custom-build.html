Custom Build

One platform. Shaped around you.

Off-the-shelf security forces you to adapt your operations to the product. We work the other way. Our engineering team takes your protocol needs, your edge applications and your operational constraints, and builds them into our FPGA-based unidirectional platform. You get a solution designed for how your environment actually runs.

01

Custom protocols on FPGA

Need NTP time synchronization, an industrial protocol or a proprietary data format to pass securely across the boundary? We implement it directly in FPGA hardware. That gives you deterministic performance and security that can’t be bypassed in software.

02

Your edge workloads, on our appliance

Run your own containerized applications and lightweight code right alongside the security layer. You can host them on the FPGA itself or on the TFG-121, so there are no extra servers or extra rack space to manage.

03

Everything in 1U

Protocol handling, unidirectional enforcement and your custom edge logic are consolidated in a single 1U TFG-121. That means less hardware, a smaller attack surface and simpler operations.
