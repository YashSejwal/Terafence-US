Why now

High-Hazard Operations Under Explicit Isolation Mandates

Recent energy cyber incidents proved how quickly enterprise breaches freeze physical throughput — from pipeline shutdowns triggered by billing-system uncertainty to a global oilfield services company's $35 million cyber disruption in 2024 and the Triton attack targeting refinery safety instrumented systems (SIS).

Regulators now demand provable separation before an incident hits. TSA Security Directive Pipeline-2021-02G requires designated pipeline operators to isolate OT so physical transport continues during an IT compromise, while USCG 33 CFR 101.650(h) requires maritime hydrocarbon terminals to document OT network segmentation in Cybersecurity Plans due July 16, 2027.

How it works

Physical Separation Without Proxy-Server Sprawl

Bidirectional firewalls between Level 3 historians and corporate IT remain one stolen credential away from lateral movement. Terafence places Side A and Side B proxy computers on opposite sides of a direction-locked FPGA core with no IP or MAC address on the data path — all in one chassis. Sessions terminate on Side A; Side B opens clean outbound sessions to IT or cloud.
