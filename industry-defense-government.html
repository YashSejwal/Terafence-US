Why now

Eliminating Lateral Pivot Paths Inside Mission Infrastructure

State-sponsored pre-positioning campaigns — detailed across joint Volt Typhoon advisories — routinely target base power microgrids, water systems, building management systems (BMS), and physical security networks to disrupt force projection and public services without crossing hardened weapons-system air gaps.

Joint CISA, DoW, DOE, and FBI guidance (Adapting Zero Trust Principles to OT, April 2026) explicitly identifies data diodes among core OT segmentation mechanisms where return traffic is unnecessary, reinforcing NIST SP 800-82 Rev. 3 and NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 (SC-7) unidirectional boundary controls. Unlike software firewalls — where rules can be misconfigured — direction-locked hardware removes the return circuit physically.

How it works

Defense-Born Isolation from Perimeter to Individual PLC

Rooted in defense cyber engineering, every Terafence one-way gateway houses isolated Side A and Side B proxy computers separated by a direction-locked FPGA core with no CPU, no OS, and no IP or MAC address on the data path. Sessions terminate on Side A; Side B opens independent outbound sessions — making hardware enforcement practical at both enclave boundaries and interior field panels.
